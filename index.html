The studio

Still Standing Studioz is the studio arm of Still Standing Records, James Albert Bosarge Jr's own label. Every song on the label is written, produced, finished, tagged, and stamped here — in-house, by one man with no staff and no shortcuts. The sound: Southern gospel rap, country-gospel, and Southern country rock, out of Alabama. James's productions are made at Still Standing Studioz — and only at Still Standing Studioz.
